Kelsey Lynn Design:

The Portfolio of

Kelsey Kendrick


Kelsey is an Edmonton designer focused on immersive experiences, story-telling, and accessible eduction.

I am a driven and passionate design and heritage professional with a reputation for creativity, a strong voice, and problem-solving. I love working with an engaged team to create great experiences for people of every ability, age and background. Service design, educational games, interpretive spaces, collaborative leadership, video editting, and community focused programs are where I shine.

Portfolio

WildTV and WildNature Reel Sampler

A sampler of five social media videos for the WildTV and WildNature broadcast channels, editted from raw footage and finalized television episodes

Pet Liscencing Accessibility

A MacEwan Service Design project in partnership with the City of Edmonton's Animal Care and Control Centre focused on increasing the accessibility of pet liscencing. Created with Raquele Callele, Ella Jane Erasmas, and Candace Mutch

Humble Haunt Tarot

Small business logo and branding for Kelsey's business as a tarot reader and deck creator inspired by Ukrainian symbolism, quilting, and women's history

The Art of the Heist

An classroom project designing an interactive exhibit around the history of art/museum theft. I created the original concept, as well as the project management, historic research, writing, designing the "is it safe" reveal and the gallery displays and lighting. Created with Victoria Lockridge, Karson Buck, and Koside Onyegbula

Your Alberta Legislature: The Story of a Place

An exhibit on the Alberta Legilsature building itself. The sections on the Legislative Chamber and Social/Celebration events were researched, written and curated by Kelsey.

Edmonton Power Historical Foundation Website

A solo class project to redesign four pages of an organizations website. I redesigned the home page, About EPHF, Power Facts, and Virtual Tours pages of the Edmonton Power Historical Foundation's site.

WildNature Promo Video

Promotional video for the WildNature broadcast channel: video editing, script writing and motion graphics

Connect the Dots Educational Game

A historical timeline matching game where students pair an Albertan event, an international event, and their coresponding year created for the Legislative Assembly of Alberta education team
